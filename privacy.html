Privacy Notice

Contact enquiries

When you use the contact form on this website, Thriven Advisory Inc. collects the information you choose to submit, such as your name, business email address, company, job title, area of interest and message.

How we use your information

We use the information you submit to review and respond to your enquiry and, where relevant, to follow up about the subject you raised. Please do not submit confidential, sensitive, financial, personal health or other restricted business information through this form.

Form processing

The contact form is delivered through FormSubmit, a third-party form processing service. Information submitted through the form is transmitted to that service and forwarded to Thriven Advisory at the business email address identified on the form. The service may process technical information needed to provide and protect its service. Please review the provider's own privacy information for details of its handling practices.

Disclosure and safeguarding

We do not use the contact form to request sensitive personal information. We take reasonable care in handling enquiries and limit use of submitted information to the purpose described above. We do not sell contact-form submissions.

Your questions

If you have a question about an enquiry you submitted or would like to request access to or correction of your information, contact geetha.kommepalli@thrivenadvisory.com.

This notice describes the website contact form only. It is not a comprehensive privacy policy for all Thriven Advisory business activities.
