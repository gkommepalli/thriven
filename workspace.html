Transformation readiness

A validated view of how work happens can inform the next questions for transformation, data governance and AI. These are potential downstream applications, not automated readiness certifications.

01 · Transformation

Process and ownership clarity

Foundation

Identify unclear handoffs, decision rights, accountability and process variation before redesign.

02 · Enterprise data

Data and system dependencies

Context

Understand where key information originates, who owns it and which applications support work.

03 · Responsible AI

Automation and agent readiness

Assessment

Surface process rules, exceptions, controls, human decisions and evidence needs before automation.

Questions to resolve before automation

Is the process understood?

Are normal paths, exceptions, triggers, inputs and outcomes documented and business-validated?

Are decision rights explicit?

Are approvals, escalation thresholds, control owners and human intervention points clear?

Is the information dependable?

Are systems of record, data ownership, quality expectations and access requirements known?

Can the outcome be governed?

Can actions be monitored, evidenced, reviewed and escalated when something goes wrong?
