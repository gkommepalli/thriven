Enterprise transformation · Practical applications

Start with business understanding. Then transform with confidence.

ERP programs, acquisitions and AI initiatives all depend on knowing how the organization actually works. Thriven is developing an AI-powered approach to help surface that knowledge, connect the moving parts and put business owners in a position to validate what is true.

Illustrative use cases · Designed for business-led discovery and validation
Where the approach can help

Three high-stakes moments when missing process knowledge becomes expensive.

These scenarios show how a connected view of processes, roles, systems, data and controls could support decisions. They describe intended applications, not customer deployments or guaranteed outcomes.

USE CASE 01

ERP & CRM transformation readiness

Before selecting, configuring or replacing enterprise systems, establish how work moves across functions and where decisions, exceptions and dependencies sit.

Common trigger
Teams have different process descriptions, undocumented workarounds, unclear ownership or conflicting requirements.

How Thriven can support the work

Bring functional perspectives into a structured discovery process, connect end-to-end activities to the people, applications, data and controls involved, and route gaps or conflicting accounts to the right business owners for review.

Clarify the current stateCapture actual activities, handoffs, decisions, exceptions and local variations across functions.
Expose dependenciesRelate processes to systems, integrations, data objects, approvals and control points.
Validate ownershipIdentify accountable process and control owners for business review and confirmation.
Inform transformation scopeProvide a traceable baseline to support requirements, design priorities and readiness discussions.
Discover work→Connect dependencies→Validate with owners→Inform design
USE CASE 02

M&A integration & separation

When organizations combine or separate, the challenge is not only systems. It is understanding which operating processes, responsibilities, data and controls must continue, change or transition.

Common trigger
Acquired or divested teams rely on undocumented processes, overlapping systems and unclear transition responsibilities.

How Thriven can support the work

Structure discovery across the affected business areas and connect process steps to legal entities, roles, applications, data flows and control responsibilities. Help teams identify questions that need resolution before transition plans are finalized.

Map operational dependenciesUnderstand cross-company handoffs, shared services, critical activities and system touchpoints.
Surface separation questionsHighlight shared data, access, integrations, vendors and activities requiring transition decisions.
Clarify accountabilityIdentify interim and target owners for process steps, services and controls.
Support transition planningGive integration leaders a structured knowledge base for sequencing, diligence and TSA discussions.
Scope entities→Map dependencies→Resolve ownership→Plan transition
USE CASE 03

Enterprise AI & agent readiness

Automation is only as dependable as the business context, decision rights, data and controls available to the agent or workflow.

Common trigger
AI opportunities are emerging, but teams cannot consistently explain the process, approved rules, exceptions or who is accountable for decisions.

How Thriven can support the work

Establish a business-validated view of the process before automation design. Separate confirmed operating rules from assumptions and unresolved differences, so AI opportunities can be assessed with appropriate human oversight.

Understand the taskCapture triggers, inputs, decisions, handoffs, exceptions and expected outcomes.
Assess information readinessConnect required data to sources, owners, quality questions and system access dependencies.
Identify governance needsSurface approval boundaries, control points, escalation paths and human decision requirements.
Prioritize candidate workflowsProvide structured process context for feasibility, risk and agent-readiness assessment.
Understand process→Validate rules→Assess readiness→Prioritize automation
A consistent foundation

One disciplined discovery approach. Different transformation decisions.

Across all three scenarios, the goal is to turn fragmented operational knowledge into a structured, reviewable picture of how the organization works.

01 · DISCOVER

Capture perspectives

Gather how work is performed from the people closest to each activity, including variations and exceptions.

02 · CONNECT

Relate the pieces

Link processes with roles, decisions, applications, data, policies and controls.

03 · RECONCILE

Surface uncertainty

Identify gaps, overlaps and conflicting perspectives for clarification rather than silently assuming an answer.

04 · VALIDATE

Confirm with owners

Keep business leaders and authorized owners accountable for reviewing and approving organizational knowledge.

Clear expectations

Useful business context, without overstating automation.

Thriven is at an early product stage. The platform is being developed to support structured discovery and process intelligence. It is not represented as a production-deployed enterprise system.
